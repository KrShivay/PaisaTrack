## 2023-10-27 - Material Touch Feedback in Custom Dashboard Widgets
**Learning:** In Paisatrack, many custom dashboard cards (like `_SetBudgetCard` in `dashboard_widgets.dart`) use `GestureDetector` wrapped around a decorated `Container`. This pattern, while functional for routing, lacks built-in visual accessibility feedback (like ripples) for standard user taps, making the interface feel less responsive compared to native OS components.
**Action:** When auditing custom interactive cards or rows, replace `GestureDetector` > `Container` trees with `Material` > `InkWell` > `Padding`. Map the `Container`'s `BoxDecoration` `color` and `borderRadius` directly to the `Material` widget, and replicate the `borderRadius` on the `InkWell` so the ripple clips to the card's bounds.
